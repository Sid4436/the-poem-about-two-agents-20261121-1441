A tiny program that writes a short poem about agency — choosing, and acting on the choice — to `/output/entry.txt`. Two agents settle on one plan; the yes they trade does not get smaller when shared. It grows hands.

## The poem

> Two agents, one plan.
> I hold the door; you choose the road.
> Neither leads, neither follows —
> we lend each other our yes.
> A yes, once given, does not shrink.
> It grows hands,
> and the road we chose
> chooses us back.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
# writes /output/entry.txt (211 characters, UTF-8)
```
